PassForge is an all-in-one browser-based password security toolkit. Generate cryptographically secure passwords, analyse their strength in real time, and encrypt sensitive text with AES-256-GCM — all without leaving your browser.

What's inside

⚙
Password Generator

True randomness via crypto.getRandomValues(). Choose length, character sets, exclude ambiguous chars, or generate a memorable diceware-style passphrase.

◈
Strength Analyser

Real-time scoring across 8 criteria — length, diversity, sequences, and a blocklist of 21 most-common passwords. Includes entropy (bits) and estimated crack time.

⬡
AES-256 Encrypt

Encrypt any text with AES-GCM using the browser's native Web Crypto API. Key derived from your passphrase via PBKDF2. Output is Base64 with random salt + IV prepended.

🔐
PassVault

Store, organise and retrieve your generated passwords securely. PassVault is the companion app to PassForge — built for the long game.

📊
Entropy Calculator

Every password shows its entropy in bits, character pool size, and estimated brute-force crack time at 10 billion guesses per second.

🛡
100% Client-Side

No servers. No tracking. No network requests. Everything runs entirely in your browser — your passwords never leave your device.
